One operational control plane

AI that can act, with controls operators can trust.

RightAPI Forge brings agent coordination, infrastructure operations, incident management, workflow automation, and internal-tool generation into the same governed runtime.

Every consequential action moves through explicit identity, policy, approval, execution, evidence, and rollback boundaries. Models assist the work; the platform owns the controls.

Governed execution lifecycle

From intent to verified outcome.

Autonomy is useful only when execution stays observable and recoverable. The platform records who requested a change, which policy allowed it, what ran, and how the result was verified.
