# Privacy Notice

Effective date: 2026-08-20

## Who We Are

RightAPI Forge is published by Shai Lukov, operating under the Right API brand. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to [info@right-api.com](mailto:info@right-api.com).

## Scope

This notice covers the RightAPI Forge project, direct sales and support communications, and project-controlled web properties. It does not replace the privacy notice of an organization that installs or operates RightAPI Forge.

RightAPI Forge is self-hosted software. Installing it does not, by itself, send operational data, user accounts, prompts, credentials, incidents, generated applications, or telemetry to Right API. The deployment operator determines what data enters its installation and is responsible for its users, purposes, lawful basis, notices, retention, and configured providers.

## Information We Receive

We may receive:

- Contact and correspondence information when a person emails sales, support, security, or licensing contacts.
- Repository account details and contribution, issue, or security-report content submitted through the project host.
- Basic web server and security logs, such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp, and error data, when a person visits a project-controlled web property.
- Contract, billing, and deployment information when a person or organization requests commercial services.

Do not send production credentials, private keys, regulated records, or unnecessary personal data through ordinary support channels.

## Purposes and Legal Bases

We use this information to respond to requests, provide requested services, secure and improve the project, manage licensing and contracts, meet legal obligations, and establish or defend legal claims. Depending on the context, the basis is performance of requested steps or a contract, legitimate interests in operating and securing the project, consent where requested, or compliance with law.

## Configured Third Parties

A deployment operator may independently configure AI providers, identity providers, notification services, observability collectors, backup storage, webhooks, and managed HTTPS integrations. Those services receive operator-selected data under the operator's configuration and their own terms. Right API is not automatically a party to those transfers.

Project communications and public repository activity may be processed by the selected email, hosting, security, and repository providers. Commercial service subprocessors will be identified in the applicable agreement or subprocessor schedule before managed processing begins.

## Retention

Support, sales, and project correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the request, relationship, security, accounting, or legal obligations. Public repository records may remain as part of project history. Self-hosted product retention is controlled by the deployment operator; documented defaults and deletion limitations are described in the product configuration and documentation.

## Sharing and International Transfers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers acting for the stated purposes, professional advisers, authorities when legally required, or a successor in a legitimate business transaction. Service providers may process information outside the person's country; required contractual or legal transfer safeguards will be used where applicable.

## Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information and service. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Security concerns should be reported as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Rights and Requests

Depending on applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and may withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing. Requests should be sent to [info@right-api.com](mailto:info@right-api.com). We may need to verify identity and may retain information where law or overriding legitimate grounds require it. A person may also complain to the competent privacy authority.

## Changes

Material changes will be recorded in this file with a new effective date. A deployment operator must provide its own notice for its installation and should not present this project notice as covering the operator's processing.
