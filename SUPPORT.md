# Support

## Contact

For installation, evaluation, licensing, and commercial support questions, email [info@right-api.com](mailto:info@right-api.com).

Include the product version or commit, deployment method, relevant timestamps, reproduction steps, expected behavior, and sanitized logs. Never send passwords, API keys, private keys, customer data, or unredacted infrastructure details by email.

## Service Levels

Community and evaluation support is provided on a best-effort basis without a response-time or resolution-time commitment. Paid support, deployment assistance, update entitlement, and service levels apply only when stated in a signed order or support agreement.

## Security

Do not report vulnerabilities through ordinary support channels when a private GitHub Security Advisory is available. Follow `SECURITY.md`; email [info@right-api.com](mailto:info@right-api.com) only when the private advisory channel cannot be used.

## Scope

Support covers the documented application and deployment configuration. Customer infrastructure, third-party APIs, model-provider availability, operating systems, networks, and integrations remain subject to their respective operators and agreements.
